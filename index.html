Built-in accounting tests

Tests check every journal entry, the trial balance, the accounting equation, the model's expected month-end figures, and correction logic.
Accounting assumptions. Fictional one-owner service business, accrual-basis accounting, USD, and no sales taxes, loans, inventory, or owner withdrawals. Equipment ($6,000, in service November 2) uses straight-line depreciation over 60 months, zero residual value, and a full-month convention: $100 November depreciation. A $1,200 three-month insurance policy covers November–January, so $400 is expensed in November. No closing entries are posted. This is a training simulation, not a replacement for professional accounting software. No data leaves your browser unless you export it.
